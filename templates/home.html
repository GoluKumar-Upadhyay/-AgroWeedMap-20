Tips for Best Results

  • Good Lighting

    Take photos in natural daylight or well-lit conditions for clearer images.

  • Clear Focus

    Ensure the plant leaf is in sharp focus and fills most of the frame.

  • Single Plant

    Photograph one plant at a time to avoid confusion in identification.

  • High Resolution

    Use images at least 500x500 pixels for optimal AI recognition.
